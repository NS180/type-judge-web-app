# ⌨️ TYPE JUDGE

### Type. Get judged. Repeat.

A typing test that judges you based on how you type.

Fast? Slow? Chaotic? Overthinking every letter?

**We'll find out. 👀**

---

## 🎮 What can you do?

- ⌨️ Take a typing test
- ⚡ See your WPM & accuracy
- 😭 Get judged based on your typing habits
- 🧠 Discover your typing personality
- 🏆 Beat your previous scores
- 😂 Try different typing modes
- 📊 Check your typing stats
- 📤 Share your result

---
